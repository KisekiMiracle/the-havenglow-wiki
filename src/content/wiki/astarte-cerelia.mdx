> A smart, sharp-tongued unorthodox wizardess specialized in close-quarters-combat and strategies.

The _**“Mad Witch”,**_ as she has been infamously known as in her home realm. Age 22. Known as a ‘spellcrafter’, she dedicates a substantial amount of her free time to studying the inner workings of magic–always trying to develop further her spells. Ended up stranded in an unknown world after a spell went wrong–or incredibly well, depending on her mood when asked about it.

Her trusty _**Spellbook**_ has been stolen by Amber and her cronies–and now the young lady is searching for their whereabouts, ready to retrieve what is hers: either the nice way, or the not-so-nice one.

## Abilities

### ◈ Manaflow Overdrive

Astarte's condition makes her mana overload and leak out of her body, which then gets channelled into her staff to empower her abilities. At the end of her turn, part of the excess mana is expelled around her, granting her and her allies' **+10% Damage Reduction** passively as long as she is in combat.

### ◈ Arcane Driver

Astarte's excess mana is stored inside her staff's core, enhancing the power of her attacks. Gains **4 MAX. HP** on Normal Attack, stacking up to 5 instances. Offensive strikes deal additional magical damage based on her current MP.

### ◉ Runic Wand

Strikes the enemy with a wand smack. Deals **100% Damage** on hit.

### ◉ Wand Reactor

The stored mana in Arcadia's core is expelled when Astarte strikes an enemy with her staff with force, dealing damage and regenerating **25% MAX. MP** on hit.

### ◉ Violent Swing

Grabbing her staff with a strong grip, Astarte delivers a powerful swing that expends a portion of her MP to deal additional magical damage onto her target. Deals **150% Damage + 75% MAX. MP \* 5.75** mixed damage.

### ◉ Guillotine Press

Astarte jumps high and then slams the ground with a completely charged wand, dealing **Massive Mixed Damage** in a small area. Gains **50% MAX. MP** upon contact.

### ◉ Blazing Surge

Astarte's Magnum Opus. Upon overcharging her body with mana, she spells it out as a fiery field that grants her and her allies **Bonus Damage** based on half of her current MP.

### ◉ Freezing Typhoon

Creating an ice vortex in front of her, Astarte pulls the frontmost enemy towards her, dealing damage along the way.

### ◉ Azure Sphere

Taking inspiration from her childhood days, Astarte creates a concentrated mana ice ball that she swings at the enemy with her staff, dealing magical damage upon contact and amplifying the damage taken by the afflicted units for 2 turns.

### ◉ Manaburst

Expels **25% of her Current MP** around, dealing magical damage to nearby enemies.
